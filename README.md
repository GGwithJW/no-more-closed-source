# Welcome to the No More Closed Source Movement.
The No More Closed Source Movement was founded by GGwithJW in 2026 regarding the closed-source resource pandemic in the FiveM community specifically relating the TMC Framework.

# Why are we doing this?
- Closed-source scripts in the TMC community have become a pain and quite frankly; AI-slop that has a lot of bugs.
- You shouldn't have to pay to not even be able to edit the resource itself.
- A lot of major TMC resources use backdoors and/or analytic loggers that tell them what servers are using their resource and can possibly include Kuzzle details.

# What is the movement's plan?
- Our plan is to recreate most known resources that get rinsed & repeated and sold closed-source, unlike competitors our resources are completely open-source and free. (p.s no ai slop either)
